We spent 12 nights in Bali this March for our honeymoon, and this is the exact plan we followed. The logic was simple: jungle and parties first, mountains next, then slow island days, and finally the cliffs near the airport so the last day is easy.

If you are planning your own trip, feel free to copy this as-is.

**When we went:** March 6 to March 18
**Route:** Ubud (3 nights) - Munduk (3 nights) - Nusa Lembongan (3 nights) - Uluwatu (3 nights)

## Phase 1: Ubud - culture, party and adrenaline

Three nights of jungle views, high-energy activities and shopping.

### Day 1 (Mar 6): Arrival

- 1:30 PM: Landed at DPS airport, cleared immigration.
- 3:00 PM: Airport pickup, pre-booked on Klook (it was cheaper than Grab). Transfer to Ubud takes about 1.5 hours.
- Evening: Checked in, got a massage at Putu's Whole Health Spa, and had dinner at Hujan Locale. A quiet local warung works just as well on arrival night.

### Day 2 (Mar 7): Art, shopping and the big night out

- 10:00 AM: Silver jewelry workshop. Pre-book one near Jl. Hanoman - you make your own ring or pendant and keep it.
- 1:00 PM: Shopping at Ubud Art Market - woven bags, macrame, wood carvings, textiles. Bargain, and take your time.
- 4:00 PM: Back to the villa for a nap. You will need it.
- 6:00 PM: Transfer to Canggu (1.5 hours).
- The party: dinner at Mexicola, drinks at Old Man's, late night at Sandbar.
- 2:00 AM: Driver takes you back to Ubud. You sleep in the car. Worth it.

### Day 3 (Mar 8): Recovery and jungle mud

- Morning: Sleep in. No alarms.
- 2:00 PM: ATV ride through the jungle around Tegallalang. Fun, muddy, and zero effort required, which is exactly what you want today.
- Evening: Early dinner, pack your bags, lights out by 9 PM. Tomorrow starts at 2 AM.

## Phase 2: Munduk - the misty mountains

Cooler air, waterfalls, and the quietest part of the trip.

### Day 4 (Mar 9): The volcano and the move

- 2:00 AM: Pickup for the Mount Batur sunrise trek.
- 6:00 AM: Sunrise from the summit, breakfast at the top.
- 10:00 AM: Back at the Ubud villa. Shower, second breakfast, pack.
- 12:30 PM: Check out and drive to Munduk (2.5 hours).
- 3:30 PM: Check in and do nothing except sit by the infinity pool. The trek has earned you this.

### Day 5 (Mar 10): The silent lake

- Morning: Tamblingan Lake canoe tour - a private traditional canoe on a still lake, usually with mist. One of the most peaceful things we did all trip.
- Afternoon: Red Coral Waterfall. An easy 15-minute walk, not a trek.

### Day 6 (Mar 11): Dolphins at dawn

- 5:00 AM: Drive to Lovina (45 minutes).
- 6:00 AM: Dolphin watching on a private boat.
- 10:00 AM: Back in Munduk. Rest of the day is completely free.

## Phase 3: Nusa Lembongan - island bliss

White sand, blue water, no agenda.

### Day 7 (Mar 12): The ocean transfer

- 9:00 AM: Drive from Munduk to Sanur harbour (3 hours).
- 1:30 PM: Fast boat to Nusa Lembongan (30 minutes).
- Sunset: Cocktails at The Deck or Ohana's.

### Day 8 (Mar 13): Manta rays and lagoons

- Morning: Private boat to Manta Bay to snorkel with manta rays. Book this - it is the highlight of the island leg.
- Afternoon: Rent a scooter or buggy, cross the Yellow Bridge, and visit the Blue Lagoon on Nusa Ceningan.

### Day 9 (Mar 14): Total relaxation

- All day: Sandy Bay Beach Club or Mushroom Bay. No plans, no timings. This day exists so the trip does not feel like a checklist.

## Phase 4: Uluwatu - cliffs and the finale

Dramatic sunsets, surf culture, and an easy exit to the airport.

### Day 10 (Mar 15): Southbound Sunday

- 11:00 AM: Boat back to Sanur, then Grab or Gojek to Uluwatu (1 hour).
- Evening: Sunday sunset at Single Fin - the iconic cliff bar. Go early for a good spot.

### Day 11 (Mar 16): Hidden beaches

- Day: Thomas Beach (much quieter than Padang Padang) or Melasti Beach.
- Dinner: Seafood BBQ at Jimbaran Bay, or a clifftop restaurant if you want to dress up once.

### Day 12 (Mar 17): The cultural send-off

- 4:30 PM: Head to Uluwatu Temple.
- 6:00 PM: Kecak Fire Dance at sunset. Touristy, yes. Skippable, no.
- Night: Farewell dinner.

### Day 13 (Mar 18): Flight home

- Morning: Sleep in, late brunch.
- 11:30 AM: Check out, Grab to the airport.
- 3:00 PM: Flight home.

## The logistics that made it work

### Transport

- **Airport pickup:** Pre-book it on Klook. It worked out cheaper than Grab for us.
- **Long transfers:** Pre-book a private driver for Ubud to Munduk and Munduk to Sanur. Expect around IDR 600k to 800k per day.
- **Short trips in Uluwatu and Sanur:** Grab and Gojek work fine and cost a fraction of hotel taxis.
- **On the islands:** Walk, rent a scooter, or take a golf cart buggy.

### Boats

Sanur to Lembongan and back: book with Rocky Fast Cruise or D'Camel. The crossing takes about 30 minutes.

### SIM and internet

Get your eSIM delivered to your hotel instead of buying an overpriced SIM at the airport. The airport wifi is good enough to book your cab and message the hotel, so you are covered until you check in.

### Apps to download before you fly

- **Gojek / Grab** for taxis and food delivery
- **Klook** for the airport pickup and activity bookings
- **WhatsApp** - every driver and hotel in Bali runs on it
- **Google Maps** - download the offline map of Bali before your flight

### One packing tip

Leave half a suitcase empty. Between the wood carvings from Ubud and everything else you pick up along the way, you will fill it. We speak from experience.
